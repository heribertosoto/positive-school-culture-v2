Why this exists

Students didn't stop caring. They stopped being asked.

New recycling bins showed up on campus with zero explanation. Students tried for a while — then watched everything get dumped into the same bag anyway. Nobody lied. Nobody explained anything either. So people stopped bothering.

"I would actually care more if I knew someone was reading what I wrote." — a student we interviewed

of students feel genuinely heard in school decisions (OECD, 2021)

How it works

Three steps. Every time.

📝

Submit

Two minutes, any campus, K–12 friendly. Say what's wrong in your own words.

📍

Track

Your case gets a number and a public status everyone can see — no black hole.

💬

Respond

A real answer within 7 school days. Adopted, adapted, or declined — you'll know why.
